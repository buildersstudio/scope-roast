# Reconcile

## Background
Six years as operations lead at Vandermeer Logistics in Rotterdam, running a team of nine. My last two years I owned freight invoice reconciliation across four carriers.

## Connection to the problem
In March 2024 we paid a duplicate carrier invoice of 41,000 euro because two people were reconciling the same batch from two copies of the same spreadsheet. I found it eleven weeks later during a quarterly close, and I had to tell our CFO.

## Ideal customer
Freight operations managers at Dutch and Belgian third-party logistics firms with 50 to 300 staff, moving 2,000 to 20,000 shipments a month. Budget sits with the finance director, on the same line that pays for their TMS.

## Quantifying the pain
Two people spent 11 hours a week each on reconciliation. We found billing errors on 3.1 percent of carrier invoices, averaging 340 euro. On 1,400 invoices a month that is roughly 14,800 euro a month recovered when we caught them, and we did not catch them all. I tracked this myself for fourteen months.

## Existing attempts
Everyone uses their TMS export plus Excel. CargoWise has a matching module that only works if the carrier files a structured EDI invoice, which our three smallest carriers never did. Transporeon handles tendering, not invoice matching. Two of my peers hired a bookkeeper in Poland to do it by hand.

## Why those fall short
TMS matching assumes the carrier invoice arrives as structured data. In this segment roughly half arrive as PDF or as a scan of paper, so the match rate collapses and the operator falls back to Excel. The incumbents will not fix it because their customers above 500 staff mostly do have EDI, so it is not their problem.

## Solution
One screen. Drop the carrier invoice PDFs and the TMS shipment export in, get a list of mismatches ranked by euro value, with the shipment reference and the discrepancy side by side. Nothing else in version one.

## Validation
Three paid pilots at 500 euro a month: Vandermeer, De Groot Transport, Hoekstra Freight. Signed pilot agreements from all three. Before that I ran the manual version by hand for De Groot for two months and found 22,000 euro of errors. Fourteen operations managers interviewed, eleven said reconciliation is in their top three time sinks.

## Distribution
I know roughly forty operations managers in this segment personally. The Dutch logistics association runs four regional events a year and I have spoken at two. First ten customers come from that network.

## Next steps
Convert the three pilots to annual contracts by December at 900 euro a month. Add two design partners from the October association event. Kill criterion: if fewer than two of three pilots convert, the pricing or the wedge is wrong and I stop and re-scope.
