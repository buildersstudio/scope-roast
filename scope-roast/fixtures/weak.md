# SynergyOS

## Background
I have 12 years of experience in technology and consulting, working with clients across financial services, retail, and healthcare on digital transformation initiatives.

## Connection to the problem
I have always been fascinated by how much time knowledge workers waste on low-value tasks. It is a problem I have seen again and again across every client I have worked with.

## Ideal customer
Any mid-sized company in Europe with more than 50 employees. Initially we will focus on SMEs but the platform is applicable to enterprises as well.

## Quantifying the pain
Studies show knowledge workers spend up to 60 percent of their time on work about work. Analysts estimate European businesses lose over 100 billion euro annually to operational inefficiency.

## Existing attempts
There are various productivity tools and workflow software solutions on the market, but none of them are truly AI-native.

## Why those fall short
Existing tools are legacy, expensive, and were not built for the AI era. They require significant manual configuration and do not learn from user behaviour.

## Solution
SynergyOS is an AI-native decision intelligence operating system that unifies workflow orchestration, knowledge management, and team collaboration into a single intelligent layer. Modules include smart routing, automated documentation, predictive resourcing, and an insights dashboard.

## Validation
I have discussed the concept with over 30 people in my network and the response has been overwhelmingly positive. We also have 240 signups on our waitlist from a LinkedIn campaign. Several competitors in this space have raised significant funding recently, which validates the market.

## Distribution
Content marketing, LinkedIn thought leadership, strategic partnerships with consultancies, and eventually a product-led growth motion.

Find a technical co-founder, build an MVP, and raise a pre-seed round. I left my job in June and I am on this full time.
