# Notes on the idea

Hospital pharmacy teams spend a lot of time chasing down which medications
came back unused from the wards, and reconciling that against what was
dispensed. I saw this constantly when I was a pharmacy technician at a large
teaching hospital for three years, mostly on the evening shift when there
was nobody to ask.

The idea is to use a camera on the returns trolley plus a model to identify
what came back, so the reconciliation is done before the trolley reaches the
dispensary.

Not sure yet who exactly buys this or how much it is worth to them. Need to
talk to more people.
